# Navakrishi — Fresh Farm Produce, Direct to You

## Introduction
Navakrishi connects local farmers with customers through a simple, trustworthy marketplace that delivers fresh, seasonal produce directly from farms. We focus on quality, transparency, and supporting sustainable agriculture.

## AI, IoT, and NLP Features
Navakrishi now includes a practical smart agriculture assistant that combines three capabilities in one workflow:

- AgriNLP Assistant: farmers can ask questions in natural language and receive crop advice, irrigation guidance, weather-aware recommendations, and symptom-based troubleshooting.
- Smart Semantic Search: marketplace searches work by meaning, so users can search with intent such as food benefits, nutrition goals, or crop use cases instead of exact product names.
- AI Product Description Generator: farmers can generate stronger product listings from a title, category, tone, and a few highlights.

The assistant also supports voice input and can surface IoT-aware responses when soil moisture or field conditions are available. This makes the platform feel like a connected AI + IoT farming tool rather than a simple chatbot.

## Example Assistant Use Cases
- "My tomato leaves are turning yellow" returns likely causes, recommended actions, and related support guidance.
- "When should I irrigate?" combines moisture signals and weather to suggest the right watering window.
- "Will it rain tomorrow?" uses weather data to help farmers avoid unnecessary irrigation.
- "Which crop is best this season?" compares temperature, humidity, and soil moisture to suggest a crop.
- "Vegetables good for diabetes" can return semantically relevant marketplace products.
- "Organic Tomato, fresh, no pesticides" can become a polished marketplace description.
